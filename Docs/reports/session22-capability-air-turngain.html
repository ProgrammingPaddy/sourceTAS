The function (lower-bound surface, v₀ = 800)

V*LB(N, Δψ) at v₀ = 800 u/s. Long horizons flatten the turning tax to nearly nothing (N=90 holds 849 u/s through 135°); short horizons pay steeply (N=6 drops 410 u/s by 180°). Witnessed structure: 1–3 reversals, hard-turn cosα ∈ [0, 0.15] during the turn phase, then pure gain — turn-then-accelerate. All six v₀ surfaces are in the CSV and the tables below.

The refutation — the program's first successful falsification

74–186 random schedules per v₀ beat the scalar surface — with witness replay and duality both green, so the instrument is sound and the conjecture is what failed. The physics: turn rate scales as 1/v, so at equal heading a slower state can out-turn-then-accelerate a faster one. Max-speed-per-heading-cell is an unsound compression; the beats grow with v₀ exactly as that mechanism predicts.

The corrected representation, and its measured cost. Speed joins the lattice — cells become (ψ bin, v stratum, side, dwell), implemented as BuildVStarStrat. The exact reachable (v, ψ) band holds 400k+ cells per layer and the std::map build hit the honest abort ceiling on all three test v₀ (falsifier beats against an aborted build are empty-layer artifacts and are not counted — the gate reads "strat build completes: FAIL" until the flat-lattice implementation lands). Cost projection: ~1.5–4B MoveTicks per v₀ single-thread (20–45 min) — the first legitimate parallelism target of the program.
