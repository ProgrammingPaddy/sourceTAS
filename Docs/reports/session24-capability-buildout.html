03The falsifier kills a certified bound

The session-20 reach cone is refuted. Its premise — "per-tick |Δv| ≤ air_speed_cap = 30, exactly" — is false for braking wishes: with the wish opposing velocity, add = cap + |v| and the accel budget (airaccelerate·wishspeed·dt ≈ 562 u/s at surf params) binds instead. Capability 2's falsifier measured 61,513 real-schedule projections beyond the cone, and the swept reach set itself holds witnessed, bitwise-replayed states 190u past the "certified" boundary (backward reach 365u vs the cone's 284u ceiling at N=60). Retroactively: B2's FFReachable / FFReachAny are demoted — nothing may hard-prune with them until re-derived.
The replacement is stronger than the loss. The correct lemma (|v_i| ≤ √(v₀² + cap²·i)) yields: a certified direction-independent D* UB (the max-speed integral, deployed and now beaten by nothing in 480,000 projections), the closed-form V* ceiling above — tight where it matters — and B6's first true certified air cull: required speed above the ceiling ⟹ impossible, with a proof.
