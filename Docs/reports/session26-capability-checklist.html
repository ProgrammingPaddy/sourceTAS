→This session's build: A11, three iterations under its own falsifiers

Version 1 (reversal family only): own-family targets 55–87% hit; general targets ~0% — proof that maximum-gain paths live on a thin arc-length shell while general fixed-tick arrivals sit deep interior (residuals up to ~1300u).
Version 2 (+ the brake run: a run of ticks with the wish opposing velocity, shedding speed to shorten the path): general coverage 0% → 13–97%.
Version 3 (+ paired-translation refinement, top-3 candidates through the exact-hit tail): machinery 92–98%, general up to 99% on long flights, at 0.4–7.0 ms per solve.
Always green: every emitted schedule dwell-legal; 145/145 solved schedules replayed through the real engine with bitwise-identical endpoints.
Honest verdict: the suite stays RED on the ≥99% machinery gate. The measured binding constraint is the initializer — coarse grids plus local climbing stall in neighboring basins — and the designed closed-form continuum initializer is the named fix, which also carries the 10–30 µs cost target (it replaces ~1,300 grid rollouts with a handful).
