01How A11 went green — a research transfer that worked

The problem is the classical bounded-curvature path problem (Dubins paths: optimal bounded-turn routes are at most three arc/straight segments — the reason our ≤3-reversal family is the right shape), with one twist: our turn radius grows with speed. The robotics motion-primitive trick then applies exactly:

under maximum gain, a constant-side run [a, b) is ALWAYS the same spiral segment (or its mirror) — so the all-plus trajectory's prefix headings and positions determine ANY reversal schedule's endpoint in O(k), with no trigonometry in the loop.
That made the candidate search exhaustive over all legal one- and two-reversal schedules and dense over three, at about 100 nanoseconds per candidate — no more basins to miss. The verified pipeline: exhaustive law enumeration → kernel verification of the top six → local refinement → the two-parameter exact-hit endgame.
