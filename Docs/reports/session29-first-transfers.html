01The permanent record, first

Per directive, the contact-epsilon law is written where laws live: as a standing law on the A18 row of the registry (Docs/CapabilityLibrary.md) and as a term in the catalog (Docs/CapabilityMethods.md) — a ridden plane re-contacts only when the inward velocity closes the 1/32-unit hover gap within one tick (velocity·normal ≤ −2.083 u/s at this tick rate); gentler inward motion produces legal airborne hover ticks.

02The ride position law, measured on the way

steady contact ticks advance position by the clipped slide over the full tick — bitwise on 992 of 1090 ticks, worst deviation 0.000035 units on the rest
The residue is the carried-gap micro-fraction: the clip's adjust pass leaves a microscopic outward velocity, drifting the hover gap until a bump lands at a tiny partial fraction. Entry ticks and post-hover re-contacts split their advance at real fractions and are boarding-event material (A24/A26), excluded from the steady law's claim. The exact carried-gap law remains the ride family's recorded refinement. capboard now carries this as its fifth gate.

03A20 — reach surfaces with a stronger witness standard

The sweep needed its own pitch: at the air-derived lattice it hit 4.2 million cells per layer and aborted honestly by layer 14 (ride speeds fan positions far faster than the air family's flights). At the ride pitch (64-unit positions, 10° headings, 50 u/s speed strata) it completes in 9–19 seconds per ramp. The semantics also grew stronger this session:

A reach bound is only published with an engine-validated witness. For each direction, the suite walks candidates from the top projection down until one replays cleanly through the engine (velocity bit-identical, position within the composed bound, never leaving the face). A direction where no candidate validates — measured: the upslope direction at 50°, whose whole extremal family consists of near-stall states hugging the contact boundary — declines its bound rather than publishing an unwitnessed number. 60° and 70° validated all eight directions with zero rejected candidates.
