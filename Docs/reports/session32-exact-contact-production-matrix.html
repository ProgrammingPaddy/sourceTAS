01The 1.8–3.7-tick systematic was a missing law, and now it is a capability

Session 31's transfer kernel landed contacts 1.8–3.7 ticks early and 13–24 units off. That was not noise — it was the hull.

The engine never traces the player as a point. Every brush plane (n, d) is pre-expanded to d + off(n), where off(n) is the Minkowski support of the negated player hull, and the trace moves the hull origin (the feet point) against that expanded set. Aiming a boarding target at the raw face plane therefore aims it below the surface the engine actually collides with — the hull's leading edge arrives first, which is exactly the early-contact signature v1 measured.

Registry A3 now packages this as CapHull::PlaneOffset, written float-identical to the world loader's own expansion (same products, same left-to-right sum, one negation). The capxfer suite checks the packaged offsets against the world's baked d_stand/d_duck/d_unduck for every plane of every brush, all three hull states: 48/48 bitwise. The face slice (A2) then simply targets the expanded plane, and the systematic vanishes.

02The contact is predicted exactly — through the engine's own clip law

With the target on the right plane, the remaining question was how precisely the composition can call the contact. The answer: exactly, because every piece of the contact is an engine truth we already hold.

The prediction pipeline (C7 v2), per solved schedule:

1 · Roll the schedule through the certified A4 air kernel (full 3-D state, ~5 µs for a 90-tick flight) and find the first tick whose motion segment crosses the hull-expanded plane — distances d1 above, d2 below.

2 · Apply the engine's trace clip law: the fraction is f = (d1 − 1/32) / (d1 − d2), which leaves the origin exactly 1/32 above the traced plane — the same 1/32 that already governs the contact epsilon and the ground rest height.

3 · The in-tick move velocity is the kernel's position delta over dt (the position law — no mid-chain state re-derived), clip it with the exported Fn::ClipVelocity, and slide the remaining (1 − f)·dt. That point is the predicted end-of-tick position.

The engine verified all of it: 63/63 contacts on the exact predicted tick, end position within 10⁻⁴ units, and the proven A18 ride law bitwise at every one of the 63 contact ticks. The session's earlier draft gated contacts with a travel-distance bound; the failures of that bound were themselves informative — slow braked arrivals shrank the bound below the residual, and two curved brake paths at 65° crossed the face plane before their requested tick. Both phenomena are now handled by prediction rather than tolerance: the kernel roll predicts the early boardings exactly too, and they are counted in the open (61/63 board on the requested tick · 2 early, predicted).

New terms-table laws. The hull offset off(n) = −Σ nᵢ·(nᵢ>0 ? minᵢ : maxᵢ) and the trace clip fraction f = (d1 − 1/32)/(d1 − d2) are recorded in the checklist's terms table — every future origin-space plane prediction consumes them, and the 1/32 family now appears in three proven places: re-contact threshold, rest height, and trace backoff.

03C6 runs at production rate — real pairs, no extrapolation

The board→exit kernel's target was set in the registry from the start: a million (boarding state, exit target) pairs in minutes. The new capmat harness runs it as production would.

Rows are engine-settled boarding states (an entry-speed × cross-slope grid, each settled into ride contact by the engine — 12/12 rows across two ramps). Per row, one A20 ride-reach surface is built, and then the full time-resolved matrix — every horizon from 4 to 48 ticks, every in-plane cell of each layer's reachable extent — is answered through the A22 indexed query.

04One label to compose on — and an honesty boundary for comparing them

Both kernels now emit the same struct at every boundary crossing, which is what the composition layer has been waiting for.

C0, CapLabel::Transition, splits its fields by role. Compatibility fields — kind, in-plane position, heading, vertical velocity, the surface-friction bit — locate where the route continues; they are never ordered. Quality fields carry certified directions only: fewer ticks (cost is ticks by decree), more s² (the coast monotonicity theorem's direction), less boundary loss (it subtracts from s² exactly). C7 emitted 63 labels at verified transfer contacts; C6 emitted 38,231 across the production matrix.

C1, CapLabel::Dominates, is deliberately cell-local: two labels are comparable only when their compatibility fields share a cell at the caller's pitch, because no monotonicity theorem in heading, vertical velocity, or position has been certified — comparing across them would smuggle an unproven claim into a prune. Within a cell, strict componentwise dominance. Property gates over the emitted set: irreflexive, zero antisymmetry violations, zero transitivity violations in 706 sampled chains. Scalarizing the tuple into one number stays banned as truth (C11: order only, never truth).
