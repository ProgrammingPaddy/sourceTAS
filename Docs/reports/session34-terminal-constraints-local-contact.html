01A14 — the brush clip, made local, stays bitwise

Route legs ask the same contact question thousands of times against the same one or two brushes. A14 gathers those brushes once and answers from the local set.

CapContact::ClipSegment is the verified full trace's per-brush loop — the enter-clamp tie-break, the DIST_EPSILON paddings, the real-side corner release, the start-solid law — transcribed operation-for-operation, so its answers are not close to the full trace's: they are the same bits. The gates confirm exactly that: 40,000/40,000 random segments across one- and two-ramp worlds return identical fraction bits, hit brush, hit plane, and start-solid flag. A segment leaving the gathered corridor is DECLINED (6,742 refusals in the narrow-corridor gate) rather than answered from an incomplete set, and 25/25 kernel-rolled flights first-contact on the same tick against the same brush as real engine replays. Queries run 60–74 ns against 113–166 ns for the full trace — and the test worlds hold only 1–2 brushes, so that gap is the floor of the locality win, not its ceiling.

02A9 — six falsifier iterations to a square Newton

The request: reach an exact point at an exact tick with terminal heading inside a tolerance. The request generator is maximally adversarial — random braked schedules' own terminal states, so every request is achievable by some schedule, usually a deep-interior one.

The iteration ledger (acceptance out of 40):

0 → 2: the exact-hit tail was overwriting brake-run ticks — stage 2 ranked candidates without the tail, stage 3 evaluated with it. The tail now sizes itself to the post-brake free region.

2 → 4: brake-to-the-end candidates had no tail room at all; shrinking the brake by four ticks buys an exact-hit tail.

4 → 6: the recorded SolveFixedN lesson applied — paired translation of the whole reversal pattern (coupled valleys stall one-at-a-time moves) plus three-start tail Newtons.

6 → 7: heading-blind endpoint climbing measurably walked candidates into far basins (endpoint 0.0u, heading 30° off); heading-only judging measurably stranded them 50u out. The coarse climb now scores BOTH contracts on every kernel roll — one roll yields endpoint and terminal heading together.

7 → 9: the brake STRENGTH (stored cosa, hardcoded 0.9 until now) became a per-candidate continuous parameter — the range-control dial with hundreds of units of authority.

9 → 19: the finisher: three continuous controls (tail c1, tail c2, brake strength) against exactly three constraints (endpoint x, endpoint y, heading) — a square 3×3 finite-difference Newton closes what discrete steps cannot.

The contracts are hard: all 19 accepts re-verified by an independent kernel roll (endpoint ≤ 0.5u, heading ≤ 5° with mean error 0.49°) and 8/8 sampled accepts replay bitwise through the engine. The 21 declines are honest — the family-sufficiency measure for this request class, and the named refinement path (a second brake run, or the A8 Pareto-layer alternative) is recorded on the row.

Why the declines are acceptable v1 semantics: a route consumer asking A9 for a boarding-quality arrival either gets a schedule whose correctness is certified twice over, or gets told NO and falls back to plain A12 — it never gets a silently-wrong answer. That is the same DECLINE discipline the ride-reach witnesses established.
