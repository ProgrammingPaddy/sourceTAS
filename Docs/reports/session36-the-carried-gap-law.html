01The boundary was a composition, not a mystery

Since session 28 the ride family carried one honest asterisk: the contact boundary. The carried 1/32 standoff and its float drift, the hover band, re-contact fractions — the conservative guards excluded it, the falsifiers measured what that excluded (beats up to +215.6 u/s), and the refinement stayed named but open.

The close is structural. CapRide::MirrorTick composes three certified pieces: the airborne chain's preamble (gravity half, clamp, the wish accel under a stateful stale surface friction — the exact categorize rule transcribed: reset to 1 every tick, probe only at vz ≤ non-jump velocity, quarter budget on rising with no walkable below), the A24 local move mirror (every trace answered by the bitwise A14 clip — entry fractions, zero-fraction steady ticks, hover misses, crease chains, the stuck guard), and nothing else. The carried gap never needed its own recurrence: it is n·pos − d_exp of a position the roller tracks bitwise.

capgap's verdict: 3600/3600 mixed contact/hover/re-contact ticks bitwise against the engine in position, velocity, and surface friction, across two ramp angles, with the boundary genuinely exercised — 842 hover ticks, 327 re-contacts. And on every tick, the generalized contact law held: a tick contacts if and only if gap + (v_move·n)·dt ≤ 0. The session-28 epsilon (re-contact needs v·n ≤ −2.083 u/s) drops out as the gap = 1/32 special case.

What this changes: boundary-surfing trajectories — the hover-mixing lines the falsifiers kept finding above the conservative bounds — are now exactly simulable without the engine. 02A22 v3 — exact hits on the ride plane, and two control laws learned

The indexed board→exit query answers in a third of a microsecond at ~30u lattice resolution. The exact-hit tail closes the rest — once the control parameterization respected two measured facts.

Measured fact one — the live band narrows with speed. A wish with true-cos beyond cap/s (~0.027 at 1100 u/s) requests more than the 30-cap allows: zero acceleration, dead control. A flat cosa parameterization wastes most of its range in that dead zone (the first Newton moved nothing: residuals ~25u, gradient ~0). Measured fact two — same-side tails are nearly one-dimensional. The authority ellipse printed almost as a line: both halves turning the same way move the endpoint along one dominant direction. The S-curve shape (second half on the opposite side — one mid-tail flip, always dwell-legal) spans the second dimension.
