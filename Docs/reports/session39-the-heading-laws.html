01Lemma one — heading is free below the budget

The air-accelerate budget B = airaccelerate · maxspeed · dt · sf = 562.5·sf is the most velocity one tick can add. Below it, that's enough to erase the heading entirely.

The full-brake wish applies a = min(30 + s, B); for s ≤ B − 30 that's 30 + s exactly, landing the velocity at precisely 30 u/s backward — and for s up to B the reversal still clears with whatever remains. The witnesses are arithmetic-exact: from s = 555, the backward speed measured 7.50 = 562.5 − 555, the budget cap showing itself digit for digit. Intermediate wishes sweep every heading between. The consequence is the scoping result that shapes everything downstream: direction-aware bounds can only improve on the blind travel integral above the budget — below it, heading costs speed but never time.

02Lemma two — the turn bound above it

Above the budget, one tick's wish adds at most B perpendicular while at least s − B of forward speed survives: tan|Δψ| ≤ B/(s − B). Sound by construction — and the falsifier says it's nearly the truth: across 200,000 adversarial one-tick actions spanning speeds to 3000, none exceeded it and the worst reached 93.3% of it. At 1400 u/s that's a hard ceiling of ~34° per tick; at 2500, ~16°. Fast flights cannot turn around quickly without paying the brake.

03The turn-gated travel bound

Displacement along a direction φ gains nothing while the heading points more than 90° away — and rotating there takes provable time.

The bound: D*_φ(N) ≤ Σ from i = T_turn to N−1 of √(s0² + 900i)·dt — the blind integrand with its first T_turn terms removed, where T_turn is the minimum heading-rotation time under the turn bound evaluated on the certified minimum-speed path (speed sheds at most B per tick). Sound for every φ; T_turn = 0 at |φ| ≤ 90° reproduces the blind bound exactly.

The measurements: at s0 1400, the backward bound is 801.7u against the blind 843.7u — a certified 5% tightening. Twenty thousand adversarial braked-and-turning schedules per configuration never beat it; their best reached only 13–39% of the bound, the honest slack line that says the certified part is the floor of what direction-awareness could someday claim, not the ceiling.

Where this feeds: the heading laws enter the terms table as first-class engine truths, gate the A8 row's direction-aware upper bound, and lay the foundation for B22's heading-aware speed ceiling when the composition layer wants it. The local-refinement alternative stays documented on-demand — no consumer has asked for it yet.
