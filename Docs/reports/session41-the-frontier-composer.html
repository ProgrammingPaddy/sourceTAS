02C9 — the min-plus composer

CapPath::MinPlusRoute is Dijkstra by accumulated ticks over C0 transition labels — but the "min" never scalarizes the state away. Per (node, compatibility cell) the composer keeps the full C1 Pareto frontier of arrivals, because a slower arrival with more retained speed can be the only one that admits the next leg. Admission is the C5 interval shape — a leg fires only from the cell its label was certified in, with at least the energy it was measured at — and relaxation is conservative: the successor takes the label's certified fields verbatim, surplus arrival energy is dropped, never extrapolated. No label ever claims more than its kernel measured.

THE LEGO RULE's routing form: every non-dominated arrival stays a first-class alternative. Dominance prunes only inside a compatibility cell, through CapLabel::Dominates — the certified quality directions and nothing else.

04The measured law behind the prune

Dominance is also termination. The first run of the prune-soundness gate failed 254/300 — and the diagnosis became a finding: on a cyclic graph, each lap re-arrives in the same cell with worse ticks and worse loss at equal speed, and the dominance prune retires it. With the prune disabled, a cyclic graph with an unreachable goal grows arrivals without bound until the pool declines. All 46 divergences were exactly that — zero genuine mismatches, zero declines on feasible graphs. The prune is not an optimization on top of the composer; it is what makes the composer terminate.
