03B22 — sound, biting where it can, honest where it can't

MaxTotalTurnUB composes the two certified s39 heading lemmas: entering each tick the speed has two certified floors (the shed floor and the gain floor), the tick's turn is at most MaxTurnUB there, and the total must reach the net heading change. The bisection over terminal speed gives the ceiling.

Where it bites, it bites completely: at 1400 u/s, a net heading reversal within 8 ticks is certified IMPOSSIBLE — the ceiling collapses to zero, and none of 120,000 adversarial schedules (random and bang-bang extremes) achieved it. The B5 cull inherits this as certified rejection.
Where it doesn't, the reason is named: at N 20–40 each lemma alone lets a fast tick also turn fast — only a JOINT law (a tick that turns θ at speed s forfeits gain) can charge turning against gaining. That joint turn-gain frontier is the recorded completion theorem, and the row stays PARTIAL by its own honesty rule until it lands.

04Three lessons banked

First: exact instruments must be compared at their own contracts — the first B11 run showed 49 "violations" of 0.1 ticks because the bound targeted the raw distance while A21 answers within its 96u lattice contract; compared honestly, the bound is clean with 1.2 ticks of slack. Second: necessary conditions can mask each other in tests — the all-rising boardability cull never fired until the test face moved inside the travel window that was culling it first. Third: arbitrary gate thresholds become measured pinned floors (B21's 16 scenarios, with the two horizon-declined air legs counted, not hidden).

The B category now stands at 21 of 23 done, B22 sound-partial, and B18 — the boundary-label dominance theorem — deliberately open: it prunes nothing until someone proves it, and its certified special case (B19, exact duplicates) already runs. With A and B under it, the C layer builds on finished foundations: C5 requirement matching on certified B13/B14, the label projections, and the first production C9 graph over B16-enumerated edges.
